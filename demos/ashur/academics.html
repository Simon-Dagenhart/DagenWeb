Academics

A well-rounded curriculum spanning our co-ed primary section (KG–Grade 6) and girls-only secondary section (Grades 7–12), built on strong fundamentals, hands-on discovery, and small class sizes.

Educational Stages

Our Educational Stages

Ashur Private Schools comprises three integrated educational stages, from kindergarten through secondary school.

Kindergarten

Comprises four phases: nursery, preparatory, kindergarten, and pre-elementary — where children learn through play and hands-on activity (co-educational).

Primary

Comprises six grades, from first through sixth primary — co-educational for boys and girls.

Secondary

Comprises six grades, from first intermediate through sixth (scientific) — for girls.

Core subjects

Our Curriculum

Reading & Writing

Balanced literacy blending phonics, guided reading groups, and daily independent writing time.

Mathematics

Hands-on, problem-based math using manipulatives before moving to abstract practice.

Science

Weekly labs and experiments covering life, earth, and physical science standards.

Social Studies

Local and world history, geography, and civics through projects and storytelling.

Art & Music

Weekly dedicated art and music instruction, plus an annual spring showcase for every grade.

Physical Education

Daily recess plus twice-weekly PE focused on teamwork, coordination, and healthy habits.

Technology

Weekly computer lab time covering typing, digital citizenship, and beginner coding (age-appropriate Scratch projects).

World Language

Introductory Spanish for all students starting in 2nd grade, twice weekly.

Support for every learner

Specialized Programs

Gifted & Enrichment

Pull-out enrichment groups and differentiated classroom projects for students ready to go deeper.

Special Education

Individualized support plans, a dedicated resource room, and inclusive co-taught classrooms.

English Language Learners

Dedicated ESL instruction alongside full participation in general education classrooms.

After-School Programs

The school day ends at dismissal; there is no after-school programme.

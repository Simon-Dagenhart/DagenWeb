About Ashur Private Schools

22 years of educating curious, kind, and confident students.

Our Mission

To provide every student with a joyful, rigorous education — from kindergarten through secondary school — that builds strong readers, curious mathematicians, and kind community members.

Our Vision

To be an inexhaustible spring of knowledge, graduating students who are confident in themselves and distinguished in their knowledge, refinement, and character — ready to reach the highest peaks.

Principal's Message

Welcome to Ashur Private Schools

Peace, mercy, and blessings of God be upon you.

Our school strives, as much as it can, to nurture within its children the seed of creativity that God has placed within the human soul — so that it may grow and flourish through diligence, just as the finest tree grows under care and, in time, yields the sweetest fruit.

To achieve this noble goal, we have spared no effort in pursuing excellence and raising our students to the level of true accomplishment — a source of pride for us. In this we hold a motto we always repeat: with the highest ambitions, we reach the highest peaks. And we strive always to make our school an inexhaustible spring of knowledge for all our students alike.

Among the timeless wisdom of the sages of China is the well-known saying:"If you plan for one year, plant wheat; if you plan for ten years, plant a tree; but if you plan for a hundred years, cultivate a human being."

So I say: true generosity is to cultivate a human being. Our students are saplings planted in the soil of life by kind hands that tended and watered them well, until they bear the finest fruit: knowledge, refinement, and good character.

In closing, I offer you my warmest wishes for progress, distinction, and excellence.

— The Administration, Ashur Private Schools

What we stand for

Our story

A Brief History

  1. 2004

    Ashur Private Schools is founded

    Opened with 120 students and six classrooms to serve a growing community.

  2. 2004

    Growing across the stages

    The school grew to include kindergarten, co-ed primary, and a girls' secondary section.

  3. 2024

    First in Iraq by number of top students

    The school earned first place across Iraq for its number of top-performing students.

  4. 2026

    40+ students with a perfect score

    More than 40 sixth-grade students achieve a perfect score, and the school remains among the top in Iraq.
